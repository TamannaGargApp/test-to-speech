FAQ

Common questions

How realistic are VoiceForge voices? +
Our neural voices are trained on thousands of hours of professionally recorded speech. In blind listening tests, listeners identify our AI voices as human over 90% of the time. We continuously improve models with every release.
Can I use generated audio commercially? +
Yes, all Pro and Enterprise plans include a full commercial license. You can use generated audio in YouTube videos, podcasts, ads, apps, and products without attribution.
What is the API rate limit? +
Starter: 5 requests/min. Pro: 50 requests/min. Enterprise: custom limits up to thousands of concurrent requests. Contact us for high-throughput needs.
Is my data used to train models? +
No. Your text inputs and generated audio are never used to train or improve our models. Enterprise plans include zero-data-retention options.
Can I cancel anytime? +
Absolutely. You can cancel your subscription at any time from your billing settings. You'll keep access until the end of your billing period. No questions asked.
